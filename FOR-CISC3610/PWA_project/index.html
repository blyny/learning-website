Learning Explorer

An interactive educational app about marine life

Features:

  • Interactive canvas with marine life illustrations
  • Educational facts about ocean creatures
  • Sound effects and audio descriptions
  • Works offline after installation
  • Installable on any device

How to Install:

  1. Open the app in Chrome or Edge browser
  2. Look for the "Install" button or the "Add to Home Screen" prompt
  3. Follow the instructions to install
  4. Enjoy learning about marine life anytime, anywhere!
